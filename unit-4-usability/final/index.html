Olive Garden Usability Report

A usability test conducted on olivegarden.com with 2 participants

Task 1: Find the Nearest Location

User 1: 5 secUser 2: 11 sec

Both users found the Find Restaurant button quickly with no struggles.

Task 2: Join the Waitlist

User 1: 8 secUser 2: 15 sec

Both users had to find a location first before the waitlist option appeared.

Task 3: Find the Tour of Italy Price

User 1: 12 secUser 2: 20 sec

Both users navigated to the menu and found the Tour of Italy price of $22.49.

Task 4: Find Fettuccine Alfredo Nutrition Info

User 1: 20 secUser 2: 27 sec

The hardest task for both users. Finding nutrition info required clicking into the item and locating a separate link.

Task 5: Sign Up for the Email List

User 1: 13 secUser 2: 19 sec

Both users had to scroll to the bottom of the homepage to find the signup form.
